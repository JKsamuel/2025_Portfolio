---
title: "Snapshotting DAX Results Without Changing Their Meaning"
summary: "Ratios, distinct counts, and time comparisons cannot always be rebuilt by summing stored rows."
publishedAt: "2025-08-24"
tag: "DAX"
---

Caching analytical results can improve response time, but a snapshot must preserve the measure's semantics. A ratio is not additive: averaging daily percentages may not equal the period percentage. A distinct count can overlap across days. Year-over-year logic depends on matched date ranges.

When publishing snapshots for station analytics, store the query scope and refresh generation alongside the values. Reuse a snapshot only when its dimensions, date range, and source refresh match the request. Keep the original measure definition as the authority and compare snapshot output with direct evaluation during validation.

The optimization boundary should be visible. If the query shape is not represented in the snapshot catalog, compute it or return a clear unsupported result rather than approximating silently. Fast numbers are useful only when they still mean what the report says they mean.
