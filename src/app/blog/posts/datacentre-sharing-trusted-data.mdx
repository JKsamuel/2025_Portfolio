summary: "An internal analytics platform needs discoverability, access boundaries, freshness, and support."
publishedAt: "2025-03-29"
tag: "Data Platform"
---

Building an internal DataCentre is more than placing reports behind a menu. Employees need to find useful data, understand what it means, know when it was refreshed, and trust that access is scoped appropriately.

At Gen7, the employee-facing DataCentre is part of how analytical information is shared. That makes product questions important: who is the audience, what decisions does each dataset support, and how should station-level data be restricted? Clear labels and consistent definitions reduce the gap between a published metric and a decision made from it.

Platform design also includes operational ownership. Someone must know when a feed is late, how a user reports a mismatch, and which source is authoritative. A DataCentre earns trust through the quality and stewardship behind each tile, not through the number of tiles on the page.
