---
title: "Bronze, Silver, Gold: A Practical Boundary Between Layers"
summary: "A medallion design is useful when each layer has a clear purpose and replay path."
publishedAt: "2023-09-04"
tag: "Azure"
---

Layer names alone do not create a reliable platform. I use them to communicate responsibility: Bronze preserves source-shaped evidence, Silver standardizes and validates it, and Gold serves a specific analytical purpose.

For example, an external report download can be retained in its original form in Bronze. A Silver transformation can normalize column names, dates, and identifiers while recording rejected rows. Gold can expose a stable table or model designed for a business question. Each step should be traceable to its inputs and safe to replay.

Not every dataset needs three elaborate copies. Storage and operational cost matter, and existing consumers may depend on current tables or procedures. A migration should inventory those dependencies before changing the architecture. The layers are a tool for managing change, not a reason to move data without a consumer or recovery plan.
